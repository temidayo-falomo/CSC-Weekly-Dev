# Ishikawa problem analysis

**Effect:** Delayed or ineffective handling of issues affecting AAUA students and host-community indigenes.

The six branches below are **candidate causes**, not findings from a completed survey. Validate them with the prepared questionnaire and, where appropriate, interviews.

| Branch | Candidate causes | How to investigate | Prototype response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People | Unclear responsible contact; reluctance to report; language or digital-literacy barriers | Awareness, barriers and language questions | Simple labelled form, explicit roles; multilingual support deferred |
| Process | Informal verbal reporting; no reference; unclear follow-up | Current channel and satisfaction questions | Reference, tracking and explicit workflow |
| Technology | Scattered channels; poor connectivity; difficult interfaces | Device and connectivity questions | Small interface with no external app dependencies; shared/offline sync deferred |
| Information | Incomplete location or description; missing category | Required-detail preferences | Structured fields and validation |
| Governance | Unclear ownership; no escalation policy; weak accountability | Interviews with proposed responsible officers | Status notes/history in demo; operational governance deferred |
| Environment | Shared infrastructure problems; distance between communities; strained relationships | Issue frequency and affected-community questions | Community/category capture; issue resolution remains an operational task |

The diagram is `../diagrams/ishikawa.drawio`. A digital interface may improve capture and visibility, but does not itself fix infrastructure, establish legitimate authority or guarantee resolution.
